About Havona Studio

Havona Studio is a free, browser-based starter studio designed for your phone.

Recordings are created in your browser. Download important takes to keep a copy. Closing or refreshing the page may remove recordings that have not been downloaded.

Live monitoring depends on your phone and browser. Bluetooth headphones can add noticeable delay. Advanced effects and AI features still need to be built.
